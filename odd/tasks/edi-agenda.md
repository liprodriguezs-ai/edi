# EDI Agenda (appointment scheduling)

Objective: turn the visit flow into a natural agenda — appointment → arrival → start of care (creates the immutable visit + folio) → next appointment suggested from the screening result.
Branch: `main`, no-git working tree (user directive for this feature: no commits/PRs). Delivery strategy: n/a (no commits). Test runners: backend PHPUnit (host PHP, test DB on 5451), frontend `tsc` + `vitest` + `pnpm build`.

## Specs

- **S1 — Appointment is its own entity; visit stays immutable.** An `appointment` (administrative) gives birth to the existing `visit` (encounter) only when care starts. The `visit` table, its folio `V-YYYY-NNNNNN`, and the walk-in-free creation from the child detail keep their current contract (no nullable folio/date). Source: approved proposal ("cita separada de la visita").
- **S2 — Appointment lifecycle (server-side FSM).** Statuses: `scheduled` (Agendada), `confirmed` (Confirmada), `arrived` (En espera), `fulfilled` (Atendida), `cancelled` (Cancelada), `no_show` (No asistió). Central catalog with fixed UUIDs. Transitions: schedule → confirm → arrive → start(fulfilled); cancel (with reason) from scheduled/confirmed/arrived (assumption: a child can leave the waiting room) and no_show from scheduled/confirmed; reschedule (move time/practitioner) only while scheduled/confirmed; terminal states frozen (409). Status and timestamps are never accepted from the client.
- **S3 — Booking.** Book from the child record or from a free slot in the day grid. **Practitioner is required at booking** (assumption: user answered "si dale apa" to "¿el médico es obligatorio al agendar, o se asigna cuando llega el niño?"). Duration defaults from the appointment type (screening / stimulation / follow-up). Start must be in the future.
- **S4 — No double booking.** The database rejects two active (scheduled/confirmed/arrived) appointments of the same practitioner whose time ranges overlap (PostgreSQL exclusion constraint). Assumption: walk-ins are excluded so an unplanned arrival never blocks a booked slot. The API answers 422 with a Spanish message.
- **S5 — "Llegó" and walk-ins.** Front desk marks arrival in one click (`arrived_at`); the practitioner is notified in-app. "Registrar sin cita" creates an appointment already `arrived` (walk-in) starting now.
- **S6 — "Iniciar atención".** From the waiting list (today, arrival order), starting care creates the visit through the existing `VisitService` (folio generated there), links it to the appointment (`fulfilled`), and opens the EDI capture for screening-type appointments.
- **S7 — Next appointment suggested from the result.** After a screening, the system proposes the next appointment date: open reassessment follow-up (amarillo) → its due date; referral (rojo) → referral, no routine re-screen; otherwise (verde) → well-child interval by age (data table seeded from NOM-031: every 2 months under 1 year, every 6 months 1–4 years, yearly after). Staff confirms with one click; never auto-booked.
- **S8 — End of day no-show.** Bulk-mark a day's still-unarrived appointments as `no_show`.
- **S9 — Agenda UI.** Day/week calendar with one column per practitioner (react-big-calendar, MIT), filter by health center, click a free slot to book, click an appointment to act (Confirmar, Llegó, Iniciar atención, Reprogramar, Cancelar, No asistió), drag & drop to reschedule. Spanish (México) UI. Menu entry "Agenda".
- **S10 — Child record + screening entry points.** Child detail: "Agendar cita" + upcoming appointments; visit rows get an "Aplicar tamizaje" action. Screening result: "Agendar próxima cita" prefilled from S7.
- **S11 — Bug fix.** On `/visit` desktop table, clicking "Aplicar tamizaje" must not also trigger the row navigation to the child.
- **S12 — Permissions/registration.** New module registered per AGENTS.md §3.9 checklist; permissions `*.appointment`; administrator gets them.
- **S13 — Health center belongs to the practitioner.** A client-sent `health_center_id` at booking/walk-in must be the practitioner's own center; otherwise 422 "El profesional no atiende en ese centro de salud.".
- **S14 — Front-desk role "Recepción" (`assistant`).** Books, confirms, marks arrival, reschedules, cancels and manages the child registry; cannot start care (no `create.visit`), screen, or delete. Assignable by the organization administrator; lands on `/agenda`.
- **S15 — Agenda opens on the user's center.** Default center: the logged-in practitioner's own center, else the only center all practitioners share, else all centers. A manual choice is remembered per browser.

Out of scope (later): weekly practitioner schedules + slot computation, recurring stimulation series, WhatsApp reminders to caregivers, caregiver self-booking.
